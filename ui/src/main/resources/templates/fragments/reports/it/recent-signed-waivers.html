Note: This report is restricted to IT Infrastructure team and Officers. It shows recently signed waivers to help identify name/email mismatches with MakerManager.

When a new member creates a MakerManager account, MakerManager validates their waiver by searching SmartWaiver for a waiver matching their first name and last name, then confirming the email matches case-insensitively. All three fields must match exactly — a typo in any one of them will cause account creation to fail. Use this report to spot mismatches between what was entered on the waiver and what the member entered in MakerManager.
